# views — EARS Specs

Specs owned by the `views` leaf (prefix `TUI-VIEW`; see `views-design.md`). Status: `[x]`
implemented · `[ ]` active gap · `[D]` deferred. `views` is the read-only TUI rendering side; it
mutates nothing and computes nothing.

## Positions / Composition

- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-001**: The system shall render Positions per symbol — shares, price, market value, pre-tax unrealized, post-tax `Net` `[est]`, Δ vs the prior trading day, a trend strip, and each row's share % of the priced portfolio.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-002**: A grouping toggle shall re-pivot Positions/Composition by symbol or platform, with per-group subtotals reflecting the full group even under an active filter.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-003**: The system shall surface the priced-coverage caveat (degraded count + priced-basis fraction) on the header, and when the priced total is ≤ 0 shall render the share column as `n/a` (in `reports`' wording), distinct from the degraded `‡` marker.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-004**: The system shall render, per Positions row, the total cost basis and the pre-tax unrealized as a percent of basis, both from `reports`' composition; basis (reconstructable) shall render even on a degraded row, while the gain-% cell shall render the `—` dash when the row is degraded and `n/a` (in `reports`' wording) when basis is ≤ 0 — distinct from the degraded `‡`.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-005**: The system shall render each symbol row's day change as $ and % from `reports`' per-symbol value delta between the latest prior captured History point and the current values — a zero-trading-day-key point shall never be selected as the prior — and shall render the `—` dash when no prior capture exists, when the symbol has no priced value at either endpoint, when the row is degraded, and on a platform group row; an incomplete prior shall degrade only the symbols it lacks, never the whole column.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-006**: The Positions summary band shall carry a `REALIZED <year> YTD` line — the current tax year's calendar-year-to-date realized P&L from `reports` — rendered with the delta sign/glyph treatment in exact whole dollars; a year with no realized sales shall render the exact flat `· $0`, never a dash or a degraded marker.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-007**: The Positions screen shall render a column title row directly above its data rows — tracked-caps titles in the fg-faint role on the same data-driven column grid as the rows (each title participating in its column's width computation under the column's clamps), with the `│` column rules at the same positions as every data row.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-008**: The Positions screen shall render the pre-tax-unrealized, gain-%-of-basis, and day-change figures in the gain/loss/flat role matching each figure's sign with the leading sign as the redundant cue, and shall render the post-tax net figures (the row's net and the summary band's `NET (POST-TAX)`) in the estimate role alongside their `[est]`-family qualifier; a degraded cell's dash keeps the dim role, never a gain/loss tint.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-009**: The Positions screen shall render, per symbol row, a company-name column resolved through `config`'s symbol→display-name map — the display name truncated with `…` only when its column clamp forces it, the ticker itself when the symbol has no map entry, and blank on a platform group row.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-010**: The Positions screen shall render the VALUE, BASIS, UNREAL, and DAY Δ cells in the compact whole-dollar form (TUI-VIEW-NAV-016), the NET cells (the row's `Net` and the summary band's figures) as exact whole dollars (never abbreviated, never with cents), and the per-share PRICE cell with two-decimal cents.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-011**: CONTRACT: the view bundle's bracket state shall be `config`'s resolved state for the current tax year (Verified / Stale / NoBracketsAvailable), threaded by the binary — so estimate figures computed under verified current-year brackets carry `[est]` and never `n/a (no brackets)`; `views` renders the bundle field and does not resolve brackets itself.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-012**: The Positions row's `Net` figure shall be the post-tax value — the row's market value less `tax`'s estimated unrealized tax, computed as basis plus `reports`' net-of-tax unrealized (the same semantics as the summary band's `NET (POST-TAX)` and `summary`'s headline Net) — so a row whose estimated unrealized tax is zero (price at or below its cost basis) renders `Net` equal to its market value, never market value × (1 − rate); the `—` dash when the row is degraded.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-POS-013**: The Positions screen shall render, per symbol row, a fixed 12-cell trend strip over the most recent twelve captured trading days — the captured points as the `▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█` ramp scaled between the window's min and max in the series' net-direction role with the latest captured cell rendered bold (the today tick), the cells for days not yet captured right-padded as dim `·` dots, an all-equal window rendering the mid rung, zero captures rendering the full 12-dot strip, and a single capture one bold mid-rung tick before its dots — and shall render no strip on a platform group row.

## Open Lots

- [x] **TUI-VIEW-LOT-001**: The system shall render Open Lots per lot (id, symbol, acquire date, source, term, remaining qty, basis, basis/share, platform, tracking code) as the drill-down target from a position.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-LOT-002**: The Open Lots screen shall render a column title row directly above its data rows — tracked-caps titles in the fg-faint role on the same data-driven column grid as the rows (each title participating in its column's width computation under the column's clamps), with the `│` column rules at the same positions as every data row.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-LOT-003**: The Open Lots screen shall render, per lot row, a company-name column resolved through `config`'s symbol→display-name map — the display name truncated with `…` only when its column clamp forces it, and the ticker itself when the symbol has no map entry.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-LOT-004**: The Open Lots screen shall render the BASIS cell in the compact whole-dollar form (TUI-VIEW-NAV-016) and the per-share $/SH cell with two-decimal cents.

## History (value-over-time)

- [x] **TUI-VIEW-HIST-001**: The system shall render value-over-time as a chart plus table over the trading-day value series (split-neutral), showing a gap as a blank cell and an incomplete day as a `‡`-tinted cell.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-HIST-002**: The system shall render degenerate series explicitly — 0 points as a no-history message, 1 point as a single tick (no trend), and an all-incomplete series with the chart suppressed and a flagged table.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-HIST-003**: The History screen shall render the value series as a multi-row block chart over the trading-day calendar — columns scaled between the series min and max, the min/max rendered as axis labels on the chart's bottom and top rows, and a date-span line beneath — tinted by net direction with the latest captured column rendered bold (the today tick); a gap day shall render a blank column, an incomplete day a `‡`-tinted (degraded-role) column, and the degenerate states of TUI-VIEW-HIST-002 shall keep their explicit renders with the chart suppressed.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-HIST-004**: The History screen shall render its min/max axis labels and table values in the compact whole-dollar form (TUI-VIEW-NAV-016).

## Tax & Reserves

- [x] **TUI-VIEW-TAX-001**: The system shall render accruals with the lifecycle stepper grouped by `(jurisdiction, tax_year)`, with reserve balances (accrued / moved / paid / outstanding / shortfall) and the next estimated-payment period, plus a provenance caveat distinguishing estimated-tax-on-gains from the gains themselves. The reserve-balance summary and the next-period figure shall stay whole-book — an active tax-year/state filter narrows only the listed accruals, never these totals. CONTRACT: the next estimated-payment period enters the view bundle from `runtime`, computed from `tax::quarterly_report` + `quarter_of(today)` (the figure `summary`'s tax line carries); `views` renders the bundle field and does not call `tax` itself.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-TAX-002**: When an accrual is selected for action, the system shall launch the matching `entry` flow passing the accrual's identity for `entry` to re-resolve live.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-TAX-003**: The Tax & Reserves screen shall render a column title row directly above its accrual rows — tracked-caps titles in the fg-faint role aligned to the accrual rows' key / amount / stepper columns.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-TAX-004**: The Tax & Reserves screen shall render accrual amounts and reserve-balance figures as exact whole dollars — never abbreviated and never with cents (reconciliation money).

## Realized

- [x] **TUI-VIEW-REAL-001**: The system shall render realized-gain history (from `reports`) by calendar year or range, labelled calendar and provenance-caveated as distinct from `tax`'s estimated-period quarterly report.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-REAL-002**: The Realized screen shall render its rows as `│`-ruled ledger columns — period · proceeds · basis · gain — with data-driven column widths, under a column title row directly above the data rows: tracked-caps titles in the fg-faint role on the same grid (each title participating in its column's width computation), the `│` rules at the same positions as every data row.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-REAL-003**: The Realized screen shall render each row's gain figure in the gain/loss/flat role matching its sign, with the leading sign and `▲`/`▼`/`·` glyph as the redundant cue.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-REAL-004**: The Realized screen shall render its proceeds, basis, and gain figures in the compact whole-dollar form (TUI-VIEW-NAV-016).

## Navigation

- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-001**: The system shall filter rows (by symbol, platform, term, state, or tax year) as visibility-only, keeping share % and totals over the whole priced portfolio, with a filter-state header (`shown/total · % of book`).
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-002**: The system shall sort by any column with a stable tie-break, sending a row to the tail only when the sort key itself is degraded for that row (in both directions).
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-003**: The system shall support drill-down (`enter` descend / `esc` ascend) on a screen stack, setting a contextual scope (a breadcrumb, cleared on ascend) over a per-screen retained user filter and sort.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-004**: The system shall anchor focus and scroll position to row identity across a refresh or re-sort, falling to the nearest row when the focused identity vanishes.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-005**: On a refresh or a concurrent `entry` append, the system shall re-resolve each stacked frame's anchor identity and replace a dangling frame with a calm returning-to-parent notice.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-006**: The system shall render six calm empty states — no positions, no open lots (`no open lots — every lot has been fully sold`), no accruals, no history, no realized gains (`no realized gains yet — they appear when you sell a lot`), and filter matched zero — each distinct from the others and from the integrity-error block.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-007**: The system shall mutate nothing; launching an `entry` action while offline or lock-held is allowed (`entry`'s write loop is the single rejection path), with the offline/lock state surfaced.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-008**: On a refresh (or a concurrent `entry` append), the system shall execute the lifecycle in a fixed order — re-read marks (or fall to the cached stale-marked set when offline) → re-resolve each stacked frame's anchor identity (TUI-VIEW-NAV-005) → re-anchor focus to the surviving row identity (TUI-VIEW-NAV-004) → re-render — so a frame never renders against half-updated state.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-009**: The system shall degrade colour down a fixed ladder by detected terminal depth — truecolor → 256 → 16 → none — mapping each role token to the nearest available representation (24-bit to nearest 256-palette index, 256 to nearest of the 16 ANSI roles, none to the always-paired glyph/word under `NO_COLOR`/`--no-color`/non-tty), preserving the redundant glyph or word at every rung.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-010**: The system shall show the active context's key hints on the status line (e.g. `[tab] entry  [/] filter  [r] refresh  [?] help  [q] quit`) — bracketed keys in the accent role, hint words in fg-faint — updating as the active context changes and hinting only keys the active context binds; while a field is in text-input mode the hints shall be suppressed per the entry keymap.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-011**: While outside text-input mode, when `?` is pressed the system shall open a modal help overlay listing the full keymap grouped by context (global / views / entry / picker), rendered as a centered panel over the current screen; `esc` or `?` shall dismiss it, and the overlay shall be chrome only — it mutates no state.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-012**: The masthead's right-aligned as-of shall render the priced trading day as a calendar date (`as-of YYYY-MM-DD`, the formatted string threaded into the view bundle by the binary), and when no priced trading day exists (no key, or a zero key) shall render `no priced day yet` — never a raw trading-day key integer and never `0`.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-013**: The status line shall carry an `updated HH:MM` segment — the wall-clock time of the run or refresh that produced the rendered view, threaded into the view bundle by the binary — and shall omit the segment (rather than fabricate a time) when no run time is available.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-014**: While in Views mode and outside text-input mode, the keys `1`–`5` shall switch directly to Positions, Open Lots, History, Tax & Reserves, and Realized respectively — replacing the screen stack with the target screen's landing frame, retaining each screen's own previously-set filter, sort, and grouping across switches (a switch never mutates anything durable).
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-015**: The Views status-line hints shall include a `[1-5] screens` hint on every Views screen (the keys are bound on every Views frame), and the help overlay shall open with a screens section naming each screen beside the key that reaches it and shall label the lot-picker group `lot picker (inside a Sell)` so the context is identifiable without prior knowledge.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-016**: The system shall format view money whole-dollar, rounding cents half-to-even; the compact form shall render magnitudes below $1,000 as whole dollars (`$842`), below $1,000,000 as tenths of a thousand (`$123.5k`), and at or above $1,000,000 as hundredths of a million (`$2.65m`), preserving the sign and delta-glyph cues; per-share money shall never compact.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-017**: While the TUI is open and no entry context is open, when one hour has elapsed since the last successful refresh (manual `[r]`, automatic, or the post-submit republish — the connect cycle that produced the opening view seeds the clock), the shell shall trigger the same refresh as the manual `[r]` binding — the full TUI-VIEW-NAV-008 lifecycle — automatically. CONTRACT: the cadence is the shell's; the Model shall read no clocks — the elapsed-time decision reaches the Model layer as data.
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-018**: While an entry context is open, the shell shall not trigger an automatic refresh; an automatic refresh that comes due during composition shall run on the first tick after the last entry context closes. An open help overlay shall not suppress an automatic refresh (the overlay is chrome over the screen it covers and holds across a refresh beneath it).
- [x] **TUI-VIEW-NAV-019**: If an automatic refresh fails, then the view shall degrade exactly as on a failed manual refresh — stale-marked offline with the last successful run's `updated HH:MM` retained, never fabricated — and the failure shall not advance the last-successful-refresh clock, so the shell retries on the next tick and recovery lands on the first tick after connectivity returns.
